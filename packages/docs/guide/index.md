# User Guide

Welcome to the SlimFact user guide. Whether you're handling your own invoices as a customer or managing everything as an administrator, here's how to get things done.

## Who is this guide for?

Choose the guide that matches your role:

### [Customer Guide](/guide/customer)
For people who receive invoices through SlimFact:
- View and pay your invoices online
- See your invoice history — bills, receipts, invoices
- Pay with iDEAL, credit card, or bank transfer

### [Administrator Guide](/guide/administrator)
For the people running the show:
- Create and manage invoices, bills, and receipts
- Set up companies, clients, and number prefixes
- Configure online payments and email templates
- Manage subscriptions and exports

---

## Quick Start

1. Head to your SlimFact instance (or [demo.slimfact.app](https://demo.slimfact.app) to explore)
2. Click **Login** and sign in with your account
3. That's it — you're in!

> **Demo access:** Visit [demo.slimfact.app](https://demo.slimfact.app) to try SlimFact with sample data. Log in with the credentials shown on the demo page.
